Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know anything about AI or automation?
No. You just need to know business owners who are drowning in manual work. We handle everything technical.
How do I track my referrals and commissions?
Every partner gets a unique referral link. We track signups, payments, and commissions. You get a monthly report.
Can I white-label SAOS as my own service?
Yes. Partners at 5+ active referrals get white-label access — your branding, your pricing, our infrastructure. Contact us for details.
What if my referred client cancels?
No commission that month. But if they come back, the commission resumes. No clawbacks on already-paid commissions.
How do I get paid?
Monthly via Stripe or PayPal. Commissions are paid on the 1st of each month for the previous month's collected revenue.
