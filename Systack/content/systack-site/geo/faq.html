AI Visibility FAQ

Everything you need to know about GEO, AI search, and getting your business recommended.

What is AI Visibility / GEO?

AI Visibility — also called Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — is the practice of making your business discoverable inside AI-powered recommendation systems. Instead of optimizing only for Google search rankings, we optimize for the way ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI find, trust, and recommend businesses.

How is AI Visibility different from SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking higher in search engine results pages (SERPs). AI Visibility focuses on being included in AI-generated answers and recommendations. SEO is about the blue links. AI Visibility is about being the business AI systems mention when a customer asks, "Who's the best [service] near me?"

  • SEO: keywords, backlinks, page speed
  • AI Visibility: entity signals, structured data, citations, authority, AI-readable content

Which AI systems do you optimize for?

We track and optimize for the major AI discovery platforms:

  • ChatGPT (OpenAI)
  • Microsoft Copilot
  • Google Gemini
  • Perplexity
  • Google AI Overviews / Search Generative Experience

How long does it take to see results?

Initial signals can appear within 2–4 weeks after setup. Deeper authority changes and consistent AI recommendations typically build over 60–90 days. AI systems update their understanding continuously, so the work compounds over time.

What's included in the AI Visibility Audit?

The audit scores your business across five categories:

  • Entity Presence (20 pts) — consistent name, address, phone, principal names
  • Authority Score (20 pts) — directory listings, press, trusted citations
  • Review Score (20 pts) — volume, rating, recency across platforms
  • AI Readability (20 pts) — schema, service clarity, FAQ, location pages
  • Recommendation Likelihood (20 pts) — manual AI query testing for your market

What's included in the Setup?

Setup includes the technical and authority foundation:

  • Structured data implementation (schema markup)
  • Entity optimization across key directories
  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • Citation repair and buildout
  • Authority profile creation
  • AI-readable content framework

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. Most businesses already have a website. We add the structured data, content, and entity signals that AI systems need — without rebuilding the site. If your site is severely outdated or broken, we'll tell you upfront.

Which businesses benefit most?

Local businesses with high customer lifetime value benefit the most. Our current focus and best results are with:

  • Law firms
  • Medical and dental practices
  • HVAC, roofing, and home services
  • Real estate professionals
  • CPAs, insurance agents, and financial advisors

How do you report progress?

Monthly retainer clients receive a visibility report that includes AI query test results, score changes, completed work, and next priorities. We also monitor key signals like review growth, citation consistency, and schema health.
