## Recommended Immediate Action Plan

### This Week (Days 1-7)

#### Day 1: LinkedIn Personal Brand (30 min)

**Post 1:** The Audit Link
```
I just built a free tool that shows small business owners exactly how 
much revenue they're losing to manual processes.

It takes 60 seconds. You get a scored report with specific gaps.

I tested it on 5 businesses. The average score was 34/100. 
One restaurant owner said it was "depressing but motivating."

If you own a business or know someone who does, try it:
https://systack.net/audit/

No email required. Just instant feedback.
```

**Post 2:** The Origin Story
```
Three years ago I was manually entering invoices into QuickBooks 
at 11 PM on a Sunday.

I built a system that now handles it automatically.

Then I built one for lead follow-up.
Then document classification.
Then reporting.

Now I help other business owners get their evenings back.

If your team is doing data entry that could be automated, 
I built something for you: https://systack.net/audit/
```

**Post 3:** The Case Study
```
Client: Restaurant owner, 3 locations
Problem: 6-8 hours/week on invoices
Solution: Automated intake, coding, approval routing
Result: "I check my dashboard over coffee and I'm done"

ROI: 267% in the first month

This is what SAOS does. Not theory. Actual deployed systems.

See if your business has similar gaps:
https://systack.net/audit/
```

#### Day 2: Reddit (2 hours)

Find posts in r/smallbusiness where people complain about:
- Manual invoicing
- Too much admin time
- Can't scale because of paperwork
- Need help with automation

**Comment template:**
```
I help small businesses automate exactly this kind of thing. 
Built a free audit tool that scores your automation readiness 
and shows where you're leaking revenue.

No pitch, just a 60-second assessment:
https://systack.net/audit/

If you score below 60, there's probably significant savings 
in automating your back-office.
```

**Important:** Add value first. Don't just drop links. Answer their question, then mention the audit as "if you want to see where you stand."

#### Day 3: Local Business Groups (1 hour)

- Little Rock Chamber of Commerce LinkedIn group
- Arkansas Small Business Association Facebook group
- Local restaurant/retail owner groups
- Industry-specific Slack/Discord communities

**Post template:**
```
Hey [group] — I built a free automation audit for small businesses.

It scores your current setup and shows you where you're losing 
hours (and revenue) to manual processes.

Takes 60 seconds. No email required.

Built it after seeing too many owners drowning in paperwork 
when they should be growing their business.

Try it: https://systack.net/audit/

Feedback welcome — especially if something doesn't make sense.
```

#### Day 4-7: Consistency

- Share audit results (anonymized) as content
- "This restaurant scored 28/100. Here's where they're losing $2,400/mo"
- Answer questions in comments
- DM anyone who engages meaningfully

## If This Doesn't Work in 2 Weeks

**Pivot options:**
1. **Paid ads** — Google Ads for "automation audit small business" ($300 test)
2. **Partnerships** — Reach out to business consultants, accountants, bookkeepers
3. **Local events** — Chamber of Commerce meetups, small business workshops
4. **Direct outreach** — Apollo.io for verified emails, rebuild cold email with real data
5. **Product-led** — Build something viral (free tool that generates shareable results)
