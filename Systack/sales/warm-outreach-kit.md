# SAOS Warm Outreach Kit
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-07-13
**Strategy:** Network-first, referral-based lead generation
**Status:** Ready to use immediately

---

## The Premise

Cold email to strangers: ~2-4% reply rate.
Warm outreach to people who know you: ~30-50% reply rate.

Your network already trusts you. They just don't know you do this.

---

## Step 1: Build Your Warm List (15 Minutes)

### Who to Contact (In Priority Order)

| Tier | Who | Why | Expected Reply Rate |
|------|-----|-----|-------------------|
| **A** | Former colleagues & bosses | Know your work ethic | 60%+ |
| **B** | Friends who own businesses | Trust + direct need | 50%+ |
| **C** | Local business owners you know | Community trust | 40%+ |
| **D** | Vendors & service providers you use | Reciprocal relationship | 35%+ |
| **E** | LinkedIn connections (active) | Weak tie but visible | 20%+ |

### How to Build It

1. **Phone contacts** — Scroll your contacts. Anyone who owns a business or manages operations?
2. **LinkedIn** — Filter by "Owner," "Manager," "Operations," "Director"
3. **Facebook/Instagram** — Friends with business pages
4. **Your email history** — Vendors, contractors, service people you've hired
5. **Local places you patronize** — Restaurant owners, gym owners, salon managers

**Target:** 20-30 names to start.

## Step 2: The Outreach Script

### Email / Text / DM Version (Casual)

```
Subject: Quick favor + a thing I'm building

Hey [Name],

Hope you're doing well — [personal note: "saw your expansion post" / "how's the new location" / "miss working with you"].

I'm building something I think could actually help [his/her/their] business. It's a back-office automation system that handles invoice processing, lead follow-up, document sorting, and reporting — basically all the stuff that eats 10-15 hours a week but isn't billable.

No pitch, just wondering: do you know any [industry] owners who are drowning in paperwork? I'm looking for 2-3 early users to test it with.

If not, no worries at all. Either way, would love to catch up sometime.

[Your name]
```

### Phone Call Version (If You're Comfortable)

```
"Hey [Name], it's [Your Name]. I'm building something I think could help 
businesses like yours — automating the back-office stuff that eats time 
but doesn't make money. Invoice processing, lead follow-up, document 
management, that kind of thing.

I'm not selling you anything. I'm looking for 2-3 businesses to pilot it 
with — get feedback, work out the kinks. Do you know anyone who might 
be a fit? Or is this something you've been dealing with yourself?"
```

### Key Principles

| ✅ Do This | ❌ Don't Do This |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Ask for referrals, not sales | Lead with pricing |
| Make it easy to say no | Send a long pitch deck |
| Add a personal note | Use generic templates |
| Offer value first | Ask for a meeting immediately |
| Follow up once, gently | Chase aggressively |

## Step 3: Handling Responses

### If They Say "Tell Me More"

Send this (keep it short):

```
Basically, I build custom automation systems that connect the tools 
businesses already use — their POS, accounting software, CRM, etc. 
The system handles the repetitive stuff: sorting invoices, following 
up on leads, generating reports, scheduling tasks.

Typical client saves 8-12 hours/week of admin time. Setup is usually 
1-2 weeks. I charge a flat monthly fee — no per-project surprises.

Worth a 15-minute call to see if it fits?
```

### If They Say "I Might Know Someone"

```
Awesome, thank you. No pressure — just send them my way if it 
comes up naturally. Here's a one-liner you can use:

"I know someone who builds automation systems for small businesses 
— handles paperwork, follow-ups, reporting. Want me to introduce you?"
```

### If They Say "Not Now / Later"

```
Totally get it. I'll check back in a couple months — or feel free 
to reach out if things change.
```

### If They Say "No Thanks"

```
No problem at all. Appreciate you considering it. Let's grab coffee 
sometime anyway — it's been too long.
```

## Step 4: The Follow-Up Cadence

| Day | Action | Message |
|-----|--------|---------|
| 0 | Initial outreach | See scripts above |
| 7 | Gentle bump | "Hey — no rush, just bumping this in case it got buried. Still looking for a couple pilot users if you know anyone." |
| 30 | Soft re-engage | "Hope things are going well. Still building — landed a couple clients, learning a ton. Would love your feedback on the direction if you ever have 10 minutes." |
| 90 | Quarterly check-in | "Quarterly update: here's what's new with SAOS. Still thinking about you — any automation pain points come up?" |

## What Makes This Work

1. **It's not a cold pitch** — it's a conversation with someone who knows you
2. **You're asking for help, not selling** — much lower pressure
3. **Referrals multiply** — one "I know someone" can turn into 3 leads
4. **Feedback loop** — even a "no" teaches you something about positioning
5. **Momentum** — first client referral is hardest; each one makes the next easier
