# SAOS Discovery Process

## Purpose
Standardized discovery framework for qualifying prospects and gathering requirements before SAOS deployment.

## Process Overview

### Phase 1: Initial Qualification (15 min)
- **Company size**: Solo, small team (2-10), growing (10-50), enterprise (50+)
- **Current pain points**: What manual tasks consume most time?
- **Tech stack**: What tools do they currently use?
- **Budget range**: Understanding of SAOS pricing tiers

### Phase 2: Deep Dive (45 min)
- **Workflow mapping**: Document current manual processes
- **Integration requirements**: Which systems need to connect?
- **Agent selection**: Which SAOS agents would provide most value?
- **Success metrics**: How will we measure ROI?

### Phase 3: Proposal & Alignment
- **Custom package**: Tailored agent selection and service tier
- **Timeline**: Deployment schedule with milestones
- **Investment**: Clear pricing with expected ROI calculation

## Discovery Questions Bank

### Business Context
1. What does your daily workflow look like?
2. Where do you spend most of your manual time?
3. What tasks do you repeat weekly?
4. What's your biggest operational bottleneck?

### Technical Context
5. What tools/platforms do you currently use?
6. Do you have any existing automations?
7. What's your comfort level with AI/ML tools?
8. Do you need integrations with specific services?

### Success Criteria
9. What would success look like in 90 days?
10. How do you currently measure productivity?
11. What's your target cost-per-lead or cost-per-task?
12. Who else needs to approve this decision?

## Output
- Completed discovery worksheet
- Recommended agent configuration
- Custom proposal with ROI projection
- Deployment timeline
