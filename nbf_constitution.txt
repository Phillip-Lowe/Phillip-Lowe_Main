Nation Basketball Federation (NBF) Constitution v1.0
Purpose
Create a persistent basketball universe where history emerges through probability, decisions, relationships, competition, and institutional pressure rather than scripted outcomes.
Core Principles
- No scripted champions, legends, villains, dynasties, or GOATs. - Basketball-only universe. - Probability-driven events. - Historical memory is permanent. - Genesis Era generates league history before public entry.
Genesis Era
The league operates through a hidden Genesis Era of approximately 50 seasons. Major milestones are preserved while routine game-level detail is discarded. Public coverage begins in Season 51.
League Structure
16 franchises. Each franchise contains ownership, front office, coaching staff, roster, reporter, fanbase, finances, arena, and historical records.
Historical Records
Championships, awards, Hall of Fame classes, retired jerseys, records, scandals, dynasties, ownership changes, and major league events are permanent.
Relationship Engine
Every major character maintains evolving relationships including allies, rivals, mentors, enemies, and trusted contacts.
Commissioner Authority
The Commissioner may fine, suspend, investigate, approve expansion, approve relocation, and propose rule changes subject to league governance.
End State Vision
The universe should naturally generate legends, dynasties, cursed franchises, Hall of Famers, retired jerseys, rivalries, scandals, and historical eras.
