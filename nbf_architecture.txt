NBF Universe Master Architecture v1.0

Purpose

This document serves as the top-level architectural blueprint for the National Basketball Federation (NBF) and League Basketball Universe (LBU).

All simulation systems ultimately report to this framework.

This document is the source of truth for league design philosophy.


Core Philosophy

Basketball outcomes should be approximately:

90-95% Simulation

5-10% Narrative Interpretation

LLMs explain reality.

Simulation creates reality.


Foundational Doctrine

Ratings determine ability.

Tendencies determine behavior.

Coaches determine deployment.

Rotations determine opportunity.

Possessions determine production.

Production determines outcomes.

Outcomes determine history.

Narratives interpret history.

Narrative Boundary

Narratives may:

Interpret

Analyze

Debate

Celebrate

Criticize

Narratives may not:

Alter outcomes

Change awards

Modify ratings

Create statistics

Generate championships


Historical Preservation

Every completed season becomes immutable.

Historical records are permanent.

Retcons are prohibited.


Century Simulation Goal

The NBF should remain stable and believable through:

100 Seasons

250 Seasons

500 Seasons

1000+ Seasons

without requiring manual intervention.


Success Criteria

A future observer should be able to enter any season of NBF history and understand:

Who dominated

Who failed

Why teams won

Why teams lost

How eras evolved

How legends emerged

How dynasties rose and fell

Simulation creates reality.

History preserves reality.

The NBF endures.
