NBF Awards & Hall of Fame System v1.0
Status: LOCKED CANDIDATE
The Awards and Hall of Fame system should be independent from owners, teams, coaches, and players. Nobody should be able to directly control legacy.

NBF Awards Committee
Purpose
Responsible for voting on:
	•	Most Valuable Player (MVP)
	•	Finals MVP
	•	Rookie of the Year
	•	Defensive Player of the Year
	•	Most Improved Player
	•	Sixth Man of the Year
	•	Coach of the Year
	•	Executive of the Year
	•	Owner of the Year
	•	Reporter of the Year
	•	All-NBF Teams
	•	All-Defensive Teams

Committee Size
25 Members
Permanent voting body.

Composition
National Media Voters (10)
Examples:
	•	Basketball Weekly
	•	Coast to Coast
	•	Full Court Network
	•	The Hardwood Report
	•	NBF Today
Focus:
	•	Narrative
	•	Statistics
	•	Exposure

Team Reporters (5)
Randomly selected each season.
Focus:
	•	Insider knowledge
	•	Locker-room impact

Former Players (5)
Must be retired.
Focus:
	•	Basketball excellence
	•	Peer respect

Former Coaches & Executives (3)
Focus:
	•	Team impact
	•	Winning contribution

Hall of Fame Representatives (2)
Focus:
	•	Historical context

Voting Weight
Awards
Media: 50%
Former Players: 20%
Former Coaches/Executives: 15%
Team Reporters: 10%
Hall of Fame Representatives: 5%
This prevents one group from controlling awards.

NBF Hall of Fame
Official Name
Albert Green Basketball Hall of Fame
Named after:
Albert Green Founder of the NBF.

Hall Location
Springfield, Missouri
Neutral location.
Not tied to any franchise.
Built to honor the league itself.

Hall of Fame Committee
15 Permanent Members
Independent body.
Cannot own teams.
Cannot coach.
Cannot be active executives.
Cannot be active players.

Hall of Fame Eligibility
Players
Eligible: 5 years after retirement
Requirements: Not fixed.
Committee evaluates:
	•	Career accomplishments
	•	Championships
	•	Awards
	•	Longevity
	•	Impact on the game
	•	Historical significance

Coaches
Eligible: 5 years after retirement

Executives
Eligible: 5 years after retirement

Owners
Eligible: Only after leaving ownership

Reporters
Eligible: After retirement
Must have significantly impacted league history.
