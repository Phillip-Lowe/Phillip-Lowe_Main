# SAOS Lead List — Template

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Primary:** Service businesses doing $500K–$5M revenue with manual processes
- HVAC, plumbing, electrical contractors
- Cleaning services (residential/commercial)
- Landscaping/lawn care
- Auto repair shops
- Small medical/dental practices
- Law firms (small)
- Real estate agencies
- Restaurants (multi-location)

**Secondary:** Any business with:
- Repetitive customer communication (scheduling, reminders, follow-ups)
- Invoice/payment collection pain
- Lead response delays
- Manual data entry between systems
