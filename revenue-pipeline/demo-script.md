## The Problem (2 min)

"Most service businesses I talk to have the same pattern:
- A customer calls or emails
- Someone has to manually check availability
- Send confirmations
- Send reminders
- Chase payment
- Follow up for review/referral

That's 6–10 touchpoints per customer. If you have 50 customers/week, that's 300–500 manual touches.

Someone quits? That knowledge walks out the door.
"

## Objection Handling

### "Too expensive"
"I get it. Here's the math: if you or your staff spend 10 hours/week on admin at $15/hr, that's $600/mo in labor. This is $299/mo and runs 24/7 without calling in sick."

### "We already have [Square/Calendly/etc.]"
"Perfect — those are point solutions. SAOS connects them. Your scheduling tool doesn't text reminders. Your invoicing tool doesn't follow up. SAOS sits between them and automates the gaps."

### "What if it breaks?"
"Every workflow has error handling and alerts. I get notified before you do. Plus, it's built on open-source tech — not some black-box SaaS that changes pricing on you."

### "I need to think about it"
"Absolutely. What specifically are you weighing? Implementation time? ROI? Something else?"
