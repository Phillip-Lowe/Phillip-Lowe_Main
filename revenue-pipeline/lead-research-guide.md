# Manual Lead Research Guide — Central Arkansas

## How to Find Real Leads (No API Key Needed)

### Method 1: Google Maps (Manual)
1. Go to maps.google.com
2. Search: "HVAC Little Rock AR" or "plumber North Little Rock AR"
3. Click each result → look for:
   - Business name
   - Phone number
   - Website
   - Reviews (3.5–4.5 stars = hungry for growth)
4. Add to `lead-database.json`

### Method 2: Yelp
1. Go to yelp.com/search?find_desc=HVAC&find_loc=Little+Rock%2C+AR
2. Filter: 10–50 reviews, 3.5–4.5 stars
3. Note: businesses with few reviews = less established online = more likely need help
4. Add to `lead-database.json`

### Method 3: Chamber of Commerce
- Little Rock Regional Chamber: https://www.littlerockchamber.com/
- Member directory = pre-qualified local businesses

### Method 4: Facebook Groups
- "Little Rock Small Business Owners"
- "Conway Area Business Network"
- Post: "Who here is drowning in scheduling/admin work?"

### Method 5: Your Network
- Ask Utopia Deli owner: "Who else do you know who could use online ordering?"
- Ask friends/family: "What business owner do you know who complains about paperwork?"

## Qualification Questions (Ask on Discovery Call)

1. "How many hours a week does your team spend on [scheduling/invoice follow-up/data entry]?"
2. "What happens when your [scheduler/office manager] is out sick?"
3. "How many leads did you lose last month to slow response?"
4. "What would you do with 10 extra hours a week?"
5. "If I could automate [specific pain point] for $299/month, would that be worth exploring?"
