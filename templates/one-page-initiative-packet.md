# One-Page Initiative Packet

**Use this for every new product or commercial initiative before the fleet builds.**

**File:** `templates/one-page-initiative-packet.md`  
**Policy:** No substantial system architecture until this packet is complete and passes the evidence gate.  
**Reference:** `memory/2026-08-02-builder-level-assessment.md`

---

## BUYER

_

## PAIN

_

## EVIDENCE
What have we directly observed?

_

## COST
What does the problem consume or prevent?

_

## CURRENT WORKAROUND
How is it handled today?

_

## THESIS
What do we believe others misunderstand?

_

## AI TRAJECTORY

_

## MINIMUM TEST
What is the smallest real-world intervention?

_

## SUCCESS

_

## STOP RULE
What evidence causes us to pause or retire it?

_

---

## Evidence Gate (check at least one)

- [ ] Three independent users show the same costly problem.
- [ ] One customer is paying for resolution.
- [ ] The system solves a documented internal bottleneck with measurable cost.
- [ ] The work is a deliberately bounded research experiment.
