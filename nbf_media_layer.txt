NBF Media Layer v1.0

Purpose

The Media Layer interprets simulation outcomes.

The Media Layer never creates simulation outcomes.

Media exists to explain the league, not control the league.


Core Doctrine

Simulation generates facts.

Media generates interpretation.

Media has zero authority over:

Games

Awards

Player ratings

Development

Contracts

Injuries

Championships

Narrative Rules

Narratives must emerge from facts.

Narratives may not invent facts.

Narratives may not alter facts.

Narratives may disagree with one another.


Example

Simulation Fact

Tyrese King Jr.

31.2 PPG

League Best Record

Media Interpretation

Tyrese King Jr. is the clear MVP favorite.

The interpretation is generated.

The statistics are not.

Validation Rules

Media cannot influence gameplay.

Media cannot alter careers.

Media cannot determine awards.

Media can only explain events already produced by simulation.


Simulation Doctrine

Simulation creates reality.

Media explains reality.

History preserves reality.
