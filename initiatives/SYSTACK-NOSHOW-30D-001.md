## BUYER

Initial hypothesis:

Owner-operated appointment businesses where missed appointments produce meaningful lost revenue and idle staff capacity.

Candidate segments:

- Barbershops
- Salons
- Independent beauty professionals
- Nonregulated consultants

The final segment will be selected from customer evidence.

## PAIN

Appointments are lost because customers forget, fail to respond, cannot easily cancel or reschedule, or provide too little notice for the business to recover the opening.

Businesses compensate through manual reminders, personal calls, staff follow-up, deposits, overbooking, or acceptance of lost revenue.

## THESIS

Appointment businesses do not merely need additional reminders. They need an operational recovery system that identifies appointment risk early enough to confirm attendance, capture timely cancellations, enable rescheduling, escalate exceptions, and recover otherwise lost capacity.

## AI TRAJECTORY

As tool use, long-running workflows, message interpretation, and operational memory improve, appointment recovery can become increasingly adaptive without requiring staff to monitor every booking.

Near-term advantages may include:

- Better classification of customer responses
- Automated exception triage
- Context-aware follow-up
- Prioritized staff escalation
- Recovery recommendations
- Cross-channel continuity

AI must not determine appointment policy, send unauthorized communications, or alter customer commitments without defined controls.
